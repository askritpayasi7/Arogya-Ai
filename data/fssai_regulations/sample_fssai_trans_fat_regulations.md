# Food Safety and Standards Regulations - Trans-Fat Limits and Claims
Standard: FSSAI Gazette Notification F. No. 1-116/Scientific Standards/Notification/2020
Regulation: Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) Regulations, Section 2.3.15
Year: 2021
Authority: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

## Statutory Maximum Permissible Limits in Fats and Oils
In exercise of the powers conferred by the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, FSSAI mandates:
1. Effective from 1st January 2021, trans fatty acid content in all edible refined oils, vanaspati, bakery shortenings, margarine, interesterified fats, and mixed fat spreads shall not exceed 3% by weight.
2. Effective from 1st January 2022 onwards, trans fatty acid content in all fats and oils shall strictly not exceed 2% by weight.

## Application to Packaged Food Products
The statutory limit of not more than 2% trans-fatty acid by weight applies universally to all processed, baked, and packaged food products where fats, oils, or hydrogenated vegetable fats are used as ingredients.

## Labelling and Trans Fat Free Claims
Under Regulation 2.2.2 (Nutrition Information Display):
1. The quantity of trans fat (in grams per 100g or 100ml or per serving) must be declared on the Nutrition Facts panel of all packaged foodstuffs.
2. A claim that a food is "Trans Fat Free" may be made on the label ONLY if:
   - The trans-fatty acid content is less than 0.2g per 100g or 100ml of the food product.
   - Saturated fat content does not exceed 1.5g per 100g or 100ml.

## Compliance and Penalty
Food Business Operators manufacturing, storing, or selling food articles exceeding the prescribed 2% TFA threshold are liable to penalties under Section 51 and Section 52 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
